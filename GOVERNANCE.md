# Governance

## Current Model

Open Helplines follows a **BDFL** (Benevolent Dictator for Life) model.
The project maintainer is [@Kouki-odaka](https://github.com/Kouki-odaka),
who holds final decision authority on direction, design, and releases.

## Decision Flow

1. **Issue** — open an issue to propose or discuss a change.
2. **Discussion** — community feedback via [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Kouki-odaka/open-helplines/discussions).
3. **Pull Request** — submit a PR referencing the issue.
4. **Review** — maintainer or trusted contributor reviews the PR.
5. **Merge** — maintainer merges once approved.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who have had **5 or more PRs accepted** and have demonstrated
consistent community trust may be invited as co-maintainers.
Invitation is at the discretion of the current maintainer.
