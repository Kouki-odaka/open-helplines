# Product Hunt Hunter Outreach Template (English)

> Usage: Send to **1 confirmed Product Hunt hunter** with 1,000+ followers in Week 0 (at least 2 weeks before target launch Thursday).  
> Goal: Secure a high-visibility hunter who will post open-helplines on Product Hunt on the designated Thursday.  
> Personalise every `[BRACKET]` before sending.

---

## What is a "hunter"?

On Product Hunt, the **hunter** is the person who submits the product. A hunter with 1,000+ followers increases discovery significantly because:

- Their followers are notified of the hunt
- Products from established hunters are weighted higher in the "Top" rankings
- It signals quality and legitimacy to first-time visitors

**You (the maker) can also be the hunter** — but a third-party hunter with a strong profile is a meaningful multiplier.

---

## Finding a hunter

Good starting points:

1. **PH leaderboard:** https://www.producthunt.com/leaderboard — filter "All time" → "Hunters"
2. **PH community on X:** Search `site:producthunt.com hunter [topic]` for AI / open-source hunters
3. **AI-focused newsletters / influencers** who also hunt on PH (common overlap)
4. **Previous hunts you've used yourself** — check who hunted them

Target profile:
- 1,000+ followers on Product Hunt
- Has hunted AI tools, developer tools, or open-data products before
- Active in the past 6 months
