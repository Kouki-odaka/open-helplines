# Safety Policy

> Full safety policy to be written by docs agent (Task #12).

## Data quality standards

All helpline records must meet the following criteria before inclusion:

1. **Verified operational**: Number/URL must be manually confirmed working.
2. **Authoritative source**: Data must cite an official or government source.
3. **No PII**: Records must not include personal contact information of individuals.
4. **Recency**: `verified_at` must be within 12 months; records older than 24 months are auto-flagged for review.

## Crisis usage disclaimer

This registry is intended for **developers and researchers** building tools to
surface mental health resources. It is not a substitute for professional crisis
intervention. Always direct end-users to local emergency services (e.g. 119/110
in Japan, 911 in the US) when life is at risk.
